How SafeVend Works

From checkout initiation to threat mitigation: how SafeVend secures micro-merchant sales without complex server infrastructure.

1

Customer Visits Artisan Store

The shopper browses handcrafted artisan inventory on CraftNest and adds items (e.g. Handmade Tote Bag for ₹999) to their cart.

↓
2

Customer Opens Secure Checkout

The checkout interface loads with the embedded SafeVend client integrity script. Input forms and hidden attributes are fingerprinted.

↓
3

SafeVend Validates Customer Input

Client inputs undergo strict sanitization and pattern checking to detect XSS injection tags, SQL probing, or malformed postal formats.

↓
4

SafeVend Verifies Transaction & Price Integrity

Submitted order totals are compared against verified catalog pricing. Any client-side price tampering (e.g., rewriting ₹999 to ₹0.01) is instantly detected.

↓
5

Threat Is Blocked & Quarantined

If tampering or automated burst anomalies are detected, the transaction pipeline is terminated before any payment gateway is contacted.

↓
6

Merchant Receives Instant Plain-Language Alert

A structured security alert with timestamp, affected component, and forensic payload difference is recorded on the Merchant Security Dashboard.

Why Existing Solutions Leave Micro-Merchants Vulnerable

Existing cybersecurity techniques can provide strong protection, but many commercial solutions remain difficult, costly, or technically demanding for resource-constrained small businesses. High subscription models ($2,000–$15,000/yr), complex cloud DNS configurations, and convoluted SIEM interfaces create an accessibility barrier. SafeVend focuses specifically on simplifying essential checkout security for micro-merchants through an ultra-low-friction, plug-and-play client verification architecture.
